MedSAM2 right-ventricle segmentation in echocardiography

At CYENS I worked on segmenting the right ventricle in echocardiography. With my supervisor, I first transferred a left-ventricle model to the right ventricle. I then extended the work on my own, using that model to prompt a fine-tuned MedSAM2, which raised mean Dice from 0.8463 to 0.8918.

  • CYENS Centre of Excellence, AI Research Engineer Intern
  • Jun 2026 to Jul 2026
  • PyTorch, MedSAM2, SLURM

Problem

Related work on automated segmentation in echocardiography is done for the left ventricle. We needed the right ventricle. MedSAM2 is a promptable model: it needs a prompt, such as a bounding box, to know where to look.

What I did

  • Transfer learning, with my supervisor: adapted a left-ventricle segmentation model to the right ventricle. This became the baseline.
  • Transferred model as prompt creator, on my own: built a PyTorch pipeline that turns its predictions into bounding-box prompts for MedSAM2.
  • Zero-shot prompt study: compared tight and padded bounding boxes, 8, 16 and 32 contour points, and box-plus-point prompts.
  • Decoder fine-tuning: ran 12 fine-tuning variants and selected a two-stage schedule: 15 epochs training only the decoder's output branch, then the full mask decoder, with the image encoder frozen.
  • Strict evaluation: 130 training, 33 validation and 41 locked cases. I chose the model on validation only and ran the locked set once.
  • Caught an invalid result: an earlier mask-prompt setup returned the input mask unchanged instead of running MedSAM2, so its scores looked good but meant nothing. I archived those experiments and kept them out of every comparison.

Results

  • 0.8463 → 0.8918mean Dice on the 41 locked cases, transfer model vs fine-tuned MedSAM2 (+4.55 points)
  • 74/82annotated frames improved
  • 0.90054mean Dice on a later, separate test set of 31 unseen cases (62 frames)
